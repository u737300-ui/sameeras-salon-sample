About Us

A Dadar favourite since 2010

Located on Madhavdas Pasta Lane in Dadar East, Sameera’s Salon & Academy has been looking after Mumbai’s hair, skin and makeup needs for over a decade — welcoming both men and women.

With a 4.8★ rating from 650 customers and a community of around 17,000 followers on Instagram (@sameerassalon), the salon is known for skilled styling, honest bundle pricing and a warm, personal touch.
